About Nesibe Org

Nesibe Org is a Turkmenistan-based EPC and industrial services company with executive-level experience delivering complex projects in the energy, infrastructure, and construction sectors.

Founded in 2005, we have grown from a specialized construction contractor into a fully integrated EPC provider — serving government entities, national oil companies, and international operators across the Caspian region.

Our success is built on our people: a team of 500+ engineers, technicians, and skilled tradespeople who bring deep local knowledge combined with international best practices to every project.
